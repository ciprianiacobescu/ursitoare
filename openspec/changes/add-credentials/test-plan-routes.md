## Canary

`CANARY` = `CANARY-5f1d9c3a-77e2-4b6c-a0d8-2b9e4c61f3a7`. On the § Route rows harness: provision `acme` with it (`k1`) and replay; `k2` duplicate; `k1` with another canary (422); rotate; `CANARY` + `\u0007`; 4097 characters starting with it; `gamma` over the closed fake and over `writer(fake.vaultDenied)`; direct calls over each writer, errors caught. Sinks (the committed document is checked in task 6.1), each one string: (1) log lines of server and writers; (2) status, headers and body of every response; (3) `name`, `message`, `JSON.stringify(details)` and `util.inspect(error, { depth: null })` (its `cause` chain included) of every thrown error; (4) `row_to_json` of every row of every table of schema `ursitoare`; (5) span names and attributes; (6) the fake's `credentials()`, `issued()`, `requests()`, `events()`; (7) the vault tokens. Control: the scanner finds `CANARY` in `"x" + CANARY` and in the request texts sent. The `request_digest` of `k1` is not `requestDigestOf({ method: "POST", route: "/api/v1/credentials", params: {}, query: {}, body: B1 with value CANARY })`.
