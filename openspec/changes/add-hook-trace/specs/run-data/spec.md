## Purpose

Run data keeps the hook trace of one node attempt on its step row, in one jsonb column that the database store and the in-memory store validate, replace and return alike.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A step row has a nullable hooks column
`src/db/migrations/0425_step_hooks.sql` SHALL add the column `hooks jsonb` (null by default) to `ursitoare.step_execution` and all its partitions, with a check that a non-null value is an object whose `entries` is an array and whose `inputOriginalRef` is JSON null or 64 lowercase hex digits, and SHALL grant `ursitoare_app` `UPDATE (hooks)` and nothing else new, so the app role still cannot update `seq` or write a leaf partition.

#### Scenario: Objects, ledger and privileges
- **WHEN** on `withTestDatabase()` with `ensurePartitions` at `2026-11-02T00:00:00Z`, tenant A holds run `r_1` and step row `n1` (`seq` 0, day `2026-11-02`); the owner reads the ledger row of version 425 and the `hooks` column of `ursitoare.step_execution`, `step_execution_default` and `step_execution_p20261102`; the app role updates `hooks` to `{"inputOriginalRef":null,"entries":[]}`, to `{"inputOriginalRef":"` + 64 times `a` + `","entries":[]}` and to `NULL`, then to `[]`, `{"entries":{}}`, `{"inputOriginalRef":null}`, `{"inputOriginalRef":"xyz","entries":[]}` and `{"inputOriginalRef":5,"entries":[]}`, and sets `seq = 5`; the owner asks `has_table_privilege` (`SELECT`, `INSERT`) and `has_column_privilege` (`hooks`, `UPDATE`) of `ursitoare_app` on both leaves, and the column privileges (`hooks`, `seq`, `UPDATE`) on the parent; `checkTenancyCatalog` runs
- **THEN** the ledger row is `{ version: 425, name: "step_hooks" }`; the column is `jsonb` and nullable on all three tables; the first three updates succeed; the five malformed ones fail with SQLSTATE `23514`; the `seq` update fails with `42501`; on both leaves the three privileges are `false`; on the parent `hooks` `UPDATE` is `true` and `seq` `UPDATE` is `false`; `checkTenancyCatalog` returns `[]`; the frozen tests `C-d540ef0d` and `C-9c1a9ff9` of `src/runs/store/schema.test.ts` pass unchanged

### Requirement: A step write carries its hook trace, kept and replaced as a whole
`recordStep` and `stepRepository.upsert` SHALL store `StepWrite.hooks` (`{ inputOriginal, entries }`) as `{ inputOriginalRef, entries }` in the `hooks` column, writing a non-null `inputOriginal` with the envelope store in the same transaction and keeping its `payloadDigest` as `inputOriginalRef`; `StepRecord.hooks` SHALL be present exactly when a trace is stored; an upsert of the same key SHALL replace `hooks`, so an absent or null `hooks` removes it; the in-memory store SHALL answer exactly as the database store and return copies.

#### Scenario: One sequence gives equal traces in both stores
- **WHEN** the sequence of test-plan.md § Store sequence runs on `createMemoryRunStore()` and on `createRunStore(db.appPool)` (tenant A, run `r_1`): a `started` write of `lookup` without `hooks`; its final write with `inputOriginal` `{ vendor_name: "  Acme " }` and one entry `pre:trim_name` (`mutated: "input"`); `pay` with six entries; `done` with none; `hooks: null`; `hooks` with no entries; `pay` again with one entry, then again without `hooks`; a failed block entry (`post:tell`, `error`, `tool`, `idemKey` all set) and a redirect entry (`to: "review"`)
- **THEN** both stores return equal lists; before the `lookup` final write `getPayload` of `364f588406d58da425488ae35b7aeb5ae04e6a5b18e212ff5c52472a33a8bd2b` is `null`, after it its `data` is `{ vendor_name: "  Acme " }`; the `lookup` record has `hooks` `{ inputOriginalRef: "364f…bd2b", entries: [<the entry>] }` and `inputRef` `1f19c0e68ba33e917a5cee0413aec2965a2fcecb63a8c97784998936bd6f67f6`; `pay` has `inputOriginalRef` `null` and the six labels in the written order; the `started` record, `done` and the `hooks: null` record have no `hooks` key; the empty trace returns `{ inputOriginalRef: null, entries: [] }`; the replacing `pay` write leaves one entry, the next one none and no key; the failed and redirect entries come back member for member; changing a returned record does not change the next `listSteps`; on the database `referencedBy` for run `r_1` and `364f…bd2b` is `false`, and for tenant B `listSteps` of `r_1` is empty and `getPayload` of `364f…bd2b` is `null`

### Requirement: A malformed hook trace is refused before anything is stored
`recordStep` and `stepRepository.upsert` SHALL throw `RunDataError` `RUN_DATA_INVALID` for `hooks` that is not exactly `{ inputOriginal, entries }`, for more than 64 entries, for an entry that lacks or adds a member of `StepHookEntry`, has a `label` outside `^(pre|post):(rule\.)?[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,127}$`, a `kind`, `state`, `action` or `mutated` outside its enum, an `attempt` that is not an integer from 1 to 2,147,483,647, a `to` that is not a node id, an `error` that is not exactly `{ code, message }` (`code` as for a run error, `message` at most 500 well-formed characters without U+0000), a `tool` that is not a block reference of at most 256 characters or an `idemKey` that is not `ik1_` and 64 hex digits, and for an `inputOriginal` that is not a payload; an `inputOriginal` over 1,048,576 bytes SHALL throw `RUN_DATA_TOO_LARGE`; a refused write SHALL change no row and store no payload.

#### Scenario: Malformed traces are refused
- **WHEN** both stores get, for node `bad`, each of the 24 cases of test-plan.md § Refusals (a `message` with U+0000, with a lone surrogate and with 501 characters; the labels `pre:Bad`, `pay/pre:rule.rule_a`, `pre:rule.rule_a:r2` and `pre:`; 65 entries; `attempt` 0, 1.5 and 2,147,483,648; `to` `Review`; `tool` `mail.send`; `idemKey` `ik1_x`; an extra and a missing member; `inputOriginal` `NaN` and one with U+0000; and others), a write with `inputOriginal` `{ leak: 1 }` and 65 entries, one with `inputOriginal` `{ s: "x".repeat(1048577) }`, and the limits of test-plan.md § Limits (64 entries, a 500-character message, `attempt` 2,147,483,647, a label with a 128-character id, then one with 129)
- **THEN** each of the 24 and the `{ leak: 1 }` write throws `RUN_DATA_INVALID`, the oversized one `RUN_DATA_TOO_LARGE`, the 129-character id `RUN_DATA_INVALID`; the limit writes are stored and come back with 64 entries, 500 characters, 2,147,483,647 and a label of 138 characters; afterwards `listSteps` holds no row of nodes `bad`, `big` or `leak`, and `getPayload` of `3df46b464a978c8002f0b17711d860a05429d9524a1a45c133b32cc7b0f3eb24` (`{ leak: 1 }`) is `null` in both stores
