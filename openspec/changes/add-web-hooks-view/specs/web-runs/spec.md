## Purpose

The run page shows the hooks that ran around a step and the steps of `code` nodes; the closing journeys of M5 prove, in a browser and against the engine, that a flow with a code node and hooks runs and that a hook route into an effect node is rejected.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A step carries its hook trace, parsed strictly
`parseStepHooks` SHALL return `undefined` for `undefined`, `null` for anything that is not `{ inputOriginalRef, entries }` with `inputOriginalRef` null or 64 lowercase hex digits and at most 64 entries of eleven valid members (`label` matching `^(pre|post):(rule\.)?[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,127}$`, `kind` `code` or `block`, `fused` boolean, `attempt` an integer of 1 or more, `state` `completed` or `failed`, `action` null or one of `continue`, `skip`, `redirect`, `retry`, `fail`, `mutated` null, `input` or `output`, `to` null or a node id, `error` null or `{ code, message }` of strings, `tool` null or a text of 1 to 256 characters, `idemKey` null or `ik1_` and 64 hex digits), and otherwise the copy; `parseStepView` SHALL keep a valid `hooks` as the last member of the view, keep a malformed one as `hooks: null` (the last member too) without refusing the step, and add no key for a step without one (`StepView.hooks` is `StepHooksView | null`: `null` means malformed, an absent key means no trace).

#### Scenario: Absent, valid and malformed hook traces
- **WHEN** `parseStepHooks` gets `undefined`, `{ inputOriginalRef: null, entries: [G, N] }`, 64 copies of `G`, and fifteen malformed values (`null`, a string, an array, no `entries`, `entries` an object, a 2-character ref, label `guard`, kind `llm`, attempt 0, attempt NaN, action `explode`, an entry with only a label, 65 entries, error `{ code: 1 }`, idemKey `ik1_x`); `parseStepView` gets `STEP`, `STEP` plus the valid `hooks`, and `STEP` plus `hooks: { entries: 5 }`
- **THEN** the results are `undefined`, a deep copy of the input (not the same objects), 64 entries, and `null` fifteen times; the first view has no `hooks` key (`"hooks" in view` is `false`); the second has the 15 keys of `STEP` followed by `hooks`, deep-equal to the input; the third has the 15 keys of `STEP` followed by `hooks` (`"hooks" in view` is `true`), its `hooks` is `null`, and its other 15 members equal the first's

### Requirement: Hook rows read like the trace
`hookRows(step)` SHALL give one row per entry of `step.hooks`, in order, and none for a step without hooks and none for a step whose `hooks` is `null` (a malformed trace): `label` the entry's label; `text` the label, ` · `, the kind plus ` (fused)` when `fused`, ` · `, the state, ` · ` and the action when it is not null, and ` → ` and `to` when it is not null; `details` the terms Attempt, Mutated (`input`, `output` or `none`), Tool (or `none`), Idempotency key (or `none`) and, for an entry with an error, Error (`code: message`). `hookTraceNote(step)` SHALL return "This step's hook trace could not be read." for a step whose `hooks` is `null` and `null` for every other step. `RunPage` SHALL show, for the chosen step with at least one row and after its details list and before "Input", a region "Hooks" (`section aria-labelledby` an `h3` "Hooks") holding a list named "Hooks" with one item per row: its `text`, then a `dl` of its `details`; for the chosen step with a note, the same region holding the note as a paragraph (not an alert, no `role`) and no list; and no region for any other step.

#### Scenario: Texts and details of the trace
- **WHEN** `hookRows` and `hookTraceNote` get `STEP` with `hooks` `{ inputOriginalRef: null, entries: [G, N] }`; a step with the entries `post:rush` (code, not fused, `redirect`, `to` `pay`), `pre:trim` (code, fused, `continue`, `mutated` `input`, attempt 2) and `post:tell` (block, `failed`, error `HOOK_ERROR` / "block hook failed (tool_failed)", tool and key as `N`); `STEP` with `hooks` `null`; and `STEP` without `hooks`
- **THEN** the first gives two rows, labels `pre:guard` and `post:notify`, texts `pre:guard · code (fused) · completed · continue` and `post:notify · block · completed`, details `[Attempt 1, Mutated none, Tool none, Idempotency key none]` and `[Attempt 1, Mutated none, Tool slack.post_message@1, Idempotency key <K>]`; the second gives the texts `post:rush · code · completed · redirect → pay`, `pre:trim · code (fused) · completed · continue` and `post:tell · block · failed`, the second with Attempt 2 and Mutated `input`, the third with the added row Error `HOOK_ERROR: block hook failed (tool_failed)`; the third and fourth calls give `[]`; `hookTraceNote` of the four steps gives "This step's hook trace could not be read." for the third and `null` for the first, second and fourth

### Requirement: Code steps have a renderer that the run page registers once
`CODE_STEP_RENDERER` SHALL be the step renderer of kind `code`: the label is the kind, plus `: ` and the outcome when there is one, and the details are Attempt and Taint (the taint joined by `, `, or `none`); `registerCodeRenderer` SHALL register it when the registry has no renderer of that kind and do nothing otherwise, and the module of `RunPage` SHALL call it. `step-renderers.ts` SHALL stay unchanged, with four built-ins.

#### Scenario: Registering and rendering a code step
- **WHEN** in a fresh process `stepRenderers.list()` and `stepRendererFor("code")` are read, `timelineItems` runs on a `code` step `normalize` with taint `["untrusted", "private"]`, `registerCodeRenderer()` runs twice, and the list, `stepRendererFor("code")`, `timelineItems`, `stepDetailRows` and `CODE_STEP_RENDERER.label` (with outcome `ok`) are read again
- **THEN** before: the kinds are `block`, `switch`, `wait`, `end`, the default renderer answers and the item text is `code (unknown kind) · completed`; after: the kinds are those four plus `code`, `stepRendererFor("code")` is `CODE_STEP_RENDERER`, the item text is `code · completed`, the detail rows end with `Attempt` 1 and `Taint` "untrusted, private" and hold no "Idempotency key", the label with an outcome is `code: ok`, and no call throws

### Requirement: The hooks view works against the real engine
The Node journey SHALL run the editor and run logic on the e2e stack's engine: a flow with a code node and hooks validates in layer `H`, runs durably in a mock test run and shows its hook trace to its author and to a team reader, and a hook route into an effect node is reported and cannot be test-run.

#### Scenario: Alice and bob run demo-hooks and alice meets the X3 rule
- **WHEN** `m5-code-hooks.test.ts` starts the stack (asserting `stack.handles.has("durable")`) and signs in alice and bob; alice creates `demo-hooks` and `demo-x3` for `finance`, saves and validates `DEMO_HOOKS` and `DEMO_X3`; starts `demo-hooks` with `INPUT` and `MOCKS_HOOKS` minus `pay:post:notify`, then with the full mocks, and follows it with `followRun`; `loadPayload` reads both payloads of `normalize` and the output of `pay`; bob runs `loadSteps`; alice starts the `DEMO_X3` version with `INPUT` and `MOCKS_HOOKS` minus `pay:post:notify` (valid mocks: the endpoint checks mocks before the version state), and `hookOverlay` runs on its `loadVersion` canonical document with the `layoutFlow` layout
- **THEN** the `demo-hooks` validation is `ok`, with no error or warning, `layers_run` `["L1","L2","L3","H"]`, version state `validated`; the first start rejects with status 422, code `TEST_MOCK_MISSING`, and `testRunFormErrors` (after `registerRunMessages()`) gives `{ mocks: MISSING + " Missing: pay:post:notify." }`; the second run ends `completed`, `terminalNode` `done`, with the steps, kinds and payloads of test-plan.md § Journey expectations, `hookRows` of `pay` giving the texts `pre:guard · code (fused) · completed · continue` and `post:notify · block · completed`, `hookTraceNote` of every step `null`, `pay.hooks.inputOriginalRef` null, the `tool` of the second entry `slack.post_message@1` and its `idemKey` matching `^ik1_[0-9a-f]{64}$`, and no `hooks` key on the other four steps; bob's steps equal alice's; the `demo-x3` validation has `ok` false, version state `draft`, exactly one error with `layer` `H`, `code` `H_ROUTE_EFFECT`, `node` `classify`, `path` `/nodes/classify/post/0/routes/0`, no warning and `layers_run` `["L1","L2","L3","H"]`; its `diagnosticRows` row has `where` "Node classify · /nodes/classify/post/0/routes/0"; its start rejects with status 422, code `OPERATION_REFUSED`, `details` `{ reason: "version_not_runnable", cause: "draft" }`; the overlay of its canonical document has the one route `{ from: "classify", to: "pay", hook: "rush" }`

### Requirement: The M5 journey proves code nodes, hooks and the X3 rejection in a browser
`e2e/test_m5_code_hooks.py` SHALL show an author validating and test-running a flow with a code node and hooks and reading its graph and hook trace, and seeing a hook route into an effect node rejected, with `assert_accessible(page)` on every page. This journey and the Node journey prove the batch outcome: flows with pure `code` nodes and pre/post hooks run durably; hook routes into effect nodes are rejected.

- **WHEN** alice signs in with `login(page, users["alice"])`, opens "Flows", creates `demo-hooks` for `finance`, fills "Flow text" with `DEMO_HOOKS`, presses "Save version" and "Validate", reads the region "Graph", fills "Input (JSON)" and "Mocks (JSON)" of the region "Test run" with `INPUT` and `MOCKS_HOOKS`, presses "Start test run", follows its one link, presses `normalize` then `pay` in the list "Steps"
- **THEN** the status in the region "Diagnostics" contains "Valid. 0 errors, 0 warnings. Layers run: L1, L2, L3, H."; the region "Graph" contains "Flow graph: 6 nodes, 5 edges, 5 layers.", its list "Hooks" has one item containing `pay: pre guard; post notify`, and it has no list named "Hook routes" or "Flow rules"; the run page shows within 20 seconds the status "Run state" "State: completed" and the buttons `lookup`, `normalize`, `route`, `pay`, `done` in order; after `normalize` the region "Step details" shows `code`, `completed`, `Attempt`, the region "Input" shows `"amount": 12.5` and `"status": "active"`, the region "Output" shows `"route": "pay"`, and there is no region named "Hooks"; after `pay` the region "Hooks" holds a list named "Hooks" of two items, the first containing `pre:guard · code (fused) · completed · continue` and the second `post:notify · block · completed`; `assert_accessible(page)` passes on each page
