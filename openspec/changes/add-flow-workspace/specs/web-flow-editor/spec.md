## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The workspace helpers split panels, list the section index and suggest owner teams
`splitPanels(panels)` SHALL return `{ side, below }` with `side` the panels whose `placement` is `"side"` and `below` every other panel (no `placement`, `"below"` or any other value), each list in the input order, and SHALL NOT change its input. `jumpLinks(panels)` SHALL return, in page order, `{ id: "editor", label: "Editor", href: "#flow-editor" }`, `{ id: "diagnostics", label: "Diagnostics", href: "#flow-diagnostics" }`, one link per side panel (`label` the panel's title, `href` `#flow-panel-<id>`), `{ id: "versions", label: "Versions", href: "#flow-versions" }`, then one link per below panel (`label` the title followed by " panel", the same `href` form). `ownerTeamSuggestions(teams)` SHALL pass each entry through `checkOwnerTeam`, keep the valid trimmed values, and return them sorted by code unit and unique as `options`, with `prefill` the only option when there is exactly one and `""` otherwise.

### Requirement: The flow page is a two-column workspace with every panel visible
The page SHALL show the section "Edit the flow" (`id` `flow-editor`: the editor, then the buttons "Save version" and "Validate" directly after it) and the section "Diagnostics" (`id` `flow-diagnostics`) in the left column and the side panels in the right column from 1280 px wide, one column below it, then the section "Versions" (`id` `flow-versions`) and the below panels side by side from 1280 px wide. Every panel SHALL be a `Section` with `id` `flow-panel-<id>` and its title as heading, and every pinned region, button and text SHALL stay rendered and visible with no tab. The header SHALL hold the `h1` `Flow <id>`, the text `Owner team: <team>`, a state `Badge` of the saved version and the editor status. Under it a `ul` named "On this page" SHALL hold the links of `jumpLinks`, stay at the top of the viewport while the page scrolls (`sticky`) and not be a `nav`. The editor box SHALL be `clamp(20rem, 100vh - 33rem, 70vh)` tall and scroll inside. The Versions table SHALL show each author's name (the id while unresolved), a `Badge` around each state, each saved time as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm UTC` inside a `<time>` that keeps the full value, and a hidden caption. "Show in the editor" SHALL leave the selected line below the index.

## MODIFIED Requirements

The editor page SHALL render every panel registered through `registerEditorPanel` after the editor and its diagnostics, in registration order within the panel's `placement` (`side` beside the editor from 1280 px wide, `below` under both, `below` by default), with the saved version and its canonical document; a panel id SHALL match `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,39}$` and a title be 1 to 60 characters. `placement` SHALL be optional, and the registry SHALL accept a panel without it and check only the id, the title and `render`.
