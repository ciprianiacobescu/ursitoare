## Purpose

The internal listener's `POST /v1/internal/audit/emit` takes the events of the two Mintkey emitters (the broker and the proxy), resolves the tenant from the emitter's token through two hash-only tenant routes, and appends each event to that tenant's audit chain with `origin` `mintkey`. It answers 2xx only after the commit. The engine holds no sink token. Reconciling the chain with Mintkey's own records is out of scope here (the observability milestone, U96).

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A sink token resolves to its tenant and the sink principal
The `service_token` verifier SHALL read the header `x-mintkey-service-token` and answer `null` (401 `UNAUTHENTICATED`) without any database read when it is absent or not 1 to 4096 characters of `\x21-\x7e`. Otherwise it SHALL call `resolveTenantBy` with the kind `MINTKEY_SINK_ROUTE_KINDS.broker` and then `MINTKEY_SINK_ROUTE_KINDS.proxy` on the PEP's pool. A match SHALL give the tenant of the route and the principal `{ tenantId, userId: "mintkey-broker" | "mintkey-proxy", kind: "service", roles: [SINK_ROLE], teams: [], actorChain: [] }`; no match SHALL answer `null` with one warn log `{ code: "AUDIT_SINK_UNBOUND" }`. The header SHALL appear in no log, response or audit row. A throwing lookup SHALL be 503 `AUTHZ_UNAVAILABLE` (the hook's answer).

### Requirement: Every wire event is mapped to an audit event or to a refusal
`mapMintkeyEvent(body, emitter, mintkeyTenantUuid)` SHALL return `{ ok: false, reason: "tenant_mismatch" }` when `body.tenant_id` differs from `mintkeyTenantUuid` (compared in lower case), `{ ok: false, reason: "event_type" }` when `body.event_type` does not match `^[a-z][a-z0-9_.]{0,63}$`, and `{ ok: false, reason: "payload" }` when the payload is nested deeper than 64 levels or `validateEvent` throws any `AuditError` for the mapped event (over 32 KiB, a NUL or lone surrogate). An absent or `null` `payload` SHALL be read as `{}`, and an absent or `null` `actor_id`, `target_id` or `target_type` as absent. Before that, every key whose name, case and punctuation ignored, contains `password`, `secret`, `token`, `apikey`, `credential`, `authorization`, `cookie`, `bearer`, `session` or `privatekey`, at any depth of objects and arrays, SHALL be removed with its value, and the removed names, once each and sorted, SHALL be the array `payload.redacted_keys` (absent when none): the audit validator refuses a secret-named key anywhere, so no placeholder can keep it. Otherwise it SHALL return `ok: true` with `eventType` the wire type, `origin` `mintkey`, `actor` `{ kind: "service", id }` where `id` is `actor_id` when it is 1 to 200 characters without control characters and `mintkey-<emitter>` otherwise, `target` `{ kind: target_type, id: target_id }` only when `target_type` matches the event-type pattern and `target_id` is such an identifier, `mk` `{ jti }` only when `payload.jti` is 1 to 200 characters of `\x21-\x7e`, and `payload` the redacted wire payload plus `redacted_keys`, `emitter` and `mintkey_actor_type` (the wire `actor_type`), which win over wire keys of the same name. A returned event SHALL pass `validateEvent`. `refusalEvent(emitter, reason, eventType)` SHALL return `audit.sink_refused`, actor `{ kind: "service", id: "mintkey-<emitter>" }`, payload `{ reason, emitter }` plus `event_type` when it matches the pattern, and nothing else of the event (no tenant id, actor, target or payload). `SINK_ROLE` SHALL be `mintkey:sink` and `SINK_REFUSAL_EVENT` `audit.sink_refused`.

### Requirement: The emit route answers 202 only after the commit
`POST /v1/internal/audit/emit` SHALL be defined by `installAuditSink` with auth `service_token` and action `emit_audit`. Its body SHALL be an object with the required strings `event_type` (1 to 200 characters), `tenant_id` (1 to 64) and `actor_type` (1 to 200), the optional strings or `null` `actor_id`, `target_id` and `target_type` (up to 512), and an optional object or `null` `payload` (absent or `null` means `{}`); further properties are allowed and ignored. A payload with secret-like keys SHALL be appended with those keys removed, never refused. A mapped event SHALL be appended with `appendAuditCommitted` in the token's tenant and answered 202 `{ "accepted": true }`; a refusal SHALL append `refusalEvent` there and answer 202 `{ "accepted": false, "reason": <reason> }`; the answer SHALL be sent after the append committed. A schema failure SHALL answer 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` and write no audit row and no decision row; a body over the server's limit 413 `PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE`. A failure of the append SHALL answer 503 `AUTHZ_UNAVAILABLE` with the standard message and one warn log `{ code: "AUDIT_SINK_STORE_FAILED" }`, writing no audit row. The tenant SHALL be the token's, never the body's. A retry after a lost answer SHALL append a second row (no deduplication in M8); each row records `mk.jti` when the payload has one.

### Requirement: A refused request is recorded once
A request the PEP denies SHALL answer 403 `FORBIDDEN` with `details.decisionId`, record one decision row and one `authz.denied` audit row, and append no event, exactly as on every route (`enforce`, with no sink-specific code). The cost of a persistent denial is bounded by that rule: one decision row and one audit row per refused request (design.md decision 7).

### Requirement: Only the sink principal may emit audit events
`src/audit/policy/audit-sink.cedar` SHALL hold the one policy `audit-sink-emit-audit`: `permit (principal in Role::"mintkey:sink", action == Action::"emit_audit", resource) when { principal.kind == "service" };`. No other principal SHALL be permitted `emit_audit`: not a service principal with any tenant role, not a human holding `SINK_ROLE`, not another tenant's sink principal, and no role a grant can hold (the role-name pattern `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,31}$` refuses `:`).

### Requirement: The sink is defined only where the role serves the API
`main` SHALL call `installAuditSink(internal, { pool: database.appPool, clock: systemClock, mintkeyTenantId })` right after `buildInternalServer` and before `deps.inspectServers`, only when `config.auditSink` is set, a database is open and `config.role` is `all` or `api`. The internal listener opens only when it has a route, so the roles `worker` and `sandbox`, a config without `auditSink` and one without an app database leave the internal port unbound. The verifier SHALL be registered once per process, so a second `main` in one process starts. An event sent by the fake egress broker or proxy with its token SHALL be in the chain when the fake answers its own caller.
