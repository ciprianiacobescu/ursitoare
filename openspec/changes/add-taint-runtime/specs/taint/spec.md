## Purpose

One taint module for the validator and the run: the per-kind output rule and the per-reference rule that `analyzeTaint` and the interpreter both call, the produced taint a run keeps for visited nodes, and the labels on step rows (the taint a node's inputs carry, which the M6 policy check reads). In the scenarios `N` is `[]`, `E` is `["extracted"]`, `U` is `["untrusted"]` and `EU` is `["extracted", "untrusted"]`.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Produced taint follows one per-kind rule for the validator and the runtime
`producedTaint(node, input, catalogue)`, exported with `referenceTaint`, `valueTaint` and `nodeInputTaint` by `src/authoring/index.ts`, SHALL return a node's output taint, the taint of each named output field and its `outcome` taint by the per-kind rule that `analyzeTaint` applied before this change, and `analyzeTaint` SHALL obtain its per-kind results from this function with every static result unchanged: for every node of every flow of the corpus of `test-plan.md` § Static agreement corpus, the node's static output, field and outcome taint SHALL equal `producedTaint(node, its static input taint, catalogue)`, and the 16 frozen tests of `test-plan.md` § Frozen taint tests SHALL pass with their files unedited.

### Requirement: References and values are tainted by their producers
`referenceTaint` and `valueTaint` SHALL give a reference or a value the join of the produced taint of what it reads and `untrusted` for whatever they cannot resolve, and `nodeInputTaint` SHALL give a node the join over its value fields plus `untrusted` when an `llm.*` or `agent` node has a context entry whose tier is not `approved`.

### Requirement: Step rows carry the effective input taint
`executeRun` SHALL write, on the `started` row of every node visit and on the final row (completed or failed) of every node visit that runs no hook, the labels of `nodeInputTaint(node, taintOf)` over the nodes this execution has already visited (no hook runs before `add-hook-runtime`), in `TAINT_LABELS` order, and a replay of recorded steps SHALL write the same labels.
